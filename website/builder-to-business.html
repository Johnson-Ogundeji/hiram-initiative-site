About the event

The final chapter of the B2B series

Builder to Business is the final chapter of The Hiram Initiative's B2B series, following Beginner to Builder, our virtual AI programme, and Beginner to Business, our in person workshop.

Across two days, we bring together founders, funders, industry experts and strategists to turn driven young builders into fully operational business owners. This is not a seminar. It is a practical accelerator of expert sessions, workshops and intensive training focused on real growth and strategic positioning.

What you will learn

Two days, five tracks

Startup Architecture

Structure and operationalise your startup to last.

Funding and Sponsorships

Position yourself to source, pitch and secure funding and partnerships.

Content as Currency

Use content and digital storytelling to market your product, build authority and attract loyal clients.

Live Workshops

Practical sessions in business model generation and video editing.

Product Showcases

See real products built by real builders, and meet the founders behind them.

Who it is for

Builders ready to become business owners

Young people aged 18 to 35, builders, creators and early stage founders ready to turn skills and ideas into a working business. Whether you are scaling a service, launching a product or positioning your brand for investment, this is your toolkit, your network and your roadmap. Completed Beginner to Builder? This is your next step.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it free?

Yes. Entry is free, but registration is required.

Do I need to attend both days?

We recommend it. Each day covers different focus areas, and together they form the full experience.

Do I need experience?

No. If you are building something, or ready to start, you will find practical value here.

What should I bring?

A laptop or smartphone for the practical workshops, and ideas you want to build on.

I missed the earlier B2B sessions. Can I still come?

Yes. Builder to Business stands on its own, and everyone is welcome.
